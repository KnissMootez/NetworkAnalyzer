title: HVC 3G subscribers with high churn in poor-network regions
tags: hvc 3g churn risk drop rate region network quality cross-db complex
question: Find HVC subscribers on 3G with churn risk above medium in regions where drop rate exceeds network average, ranked by ARPU
db: BOTH
sql: ATTACH DATABASE 'operator_new.db' AS op; WITH region_drop AS (SELECT si.region, AVG(k.dropped_call_rate) AS avg_drop FROM kpis_daily k JOIN cells c ON k.cell_id=c.cell_id JOIN sites si ON c.site_id=si.site_id WHERE k.date=(SELECT MAX(date) FROM kpis_daily) GROUP BY si.region), net_avg AS (SELECT AVG(dropped_call_rate) AS avg FROM kpis_daily WHERE date=(SELECT MAX(date) FROM kpis_daily)) SELECT cv.msisdn, cv.arpu, cv.churn_risk_score, cv.churn_label, s.region, st.current_technology, ROUND(rd.avg_drop,4) AS region_drop_rate FROM op.customer_value cv JOIN subscribers s ON cv.msisdn=s.msisdn JOIN subscriber_technology st ON cv.msisdn=st.msisdn JOIN region_drop rd ON s.region=rd.region CROSS JOIN net_avg na WHERE cv.month=(SELECT MAX(month) FROM op.customer_value) AND cv.is_hvc=1 AND st.current_technology='3G' AND cv.churn_label IN ('medium','high') AND rd.avg_drop > na.avg ORDER BY cv.arpu DESC
note: ALWAYS go kpis_daily→cells→sites to get region. Column is dropped_call_rate not drop_rate. churn_label values are 'low','medium','high'. Use churn_label IN ('medium','high') for above-medium risk.
==END==

title: Churn risk — high data users who dropped usage (month-over-month from dou_monthly)
tags: churn risk data usage drop decline trend monthly dou_monthly high low users plan
question: Identify subscribers who were high data users 3 months ago but low usage last month
db: BOTH
sql: ATTACH DATABASE 'operator_new.db' AS op; WITH last_mo AS (SELECT msisdn, total_data_gb FROM dou_monthly WHERE month=(SELECT MAX(month) FROM dou_monthly)), three_mo_ago AS (SELECT msisdn, total_data_gb FROM dou_monthly WHERE month=(SELECT month FROM (SELECT DISTINCT month FROM dou_monthly ORDER BY month DESC LIMIT 1 OFFSET 2) t)) SELECT s.region, COUNT(DISTINCT t.msisdn) as churn_risk_count, ROUND(AVG(t.total_data_gb),1) as avg_usage_3mo_ago, ROUND(AVG(l.total_data_gb),1) as avg_usage_last_mo, GROUP_CONCAT(DISTINCT p.plan_name) as plans FROM three_mo_ago t JOIN last_mo l ON t.msisdn=l.msisdn AND t.total_data_gb > 30 AND l.total_data_gb < 10 JOIN subscribers s ON t.msisdn=s.msisdn JOIN op.subscriptions sub ON t.msisdn=sub.msisdn AND sub.is_current=1 JOIN op.plans p ON sub.plan_id=p.plan_id GROUP BY s.region ORDER BY churn_risk_count DESC
note: Use WITH CTEs to isolate each month's data first, then JOIN on msisdn. dou_monthly is in SC DB; plans/subscriptions are in op DB — always use QUERY_BOTH with ATTACH. NEVER use subscriber_technology for usage — it has no usage columns.

title: Campaign performance — sent, delivered, conversion and opt-out rates
tags: campaign performance results outcome conversion opt-out delivered sms how did campaign perform funnel
question: How did our campaigns perform? Show conversion and opt-out rates per campaign.
db: OP
sql: SELECT c.campaign_id, c.campaign_name, c.campaign_type, c.status, COUNT(sl.sms_id) AS sms_sent, SUM(CASE WHEN sl.status='delivered' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS delivered, SUM(CASE WHEN sl.response='opt_in' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS opt_in, SUM(CASE WHEN sl.response='opt_out' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS opt_out, SUM(CASE WHEN sl.response='query' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS queries, ROUND(100.0*SUM(CASE WHEN sl.response='opt_in' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)/NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN sl.status='delivered' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END),0),1) AS conversion_pct FROM campaigns c LEFT JOIN sms_log sl ON c.campaign_id=sl.campaign_id GROUP BY c.campaign_id ORDER BY conversion_pct DESC
note: Campaign feedback lives in sms_log (operator DB): status is sent/delivered/failed, response is none/opt_in/opt_out/query. opt_in = converted. Conversion rate = opt_in / delivered. campaign_targets.converted mirrors opt_in.
